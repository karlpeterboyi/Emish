Privacy Policy

Emish Beauty Spa (“Emish”, “we”, “us”) operates the website emish.store, related booking and shop services, and WhatsApp messaging for customers in Tanzania. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we use it, and your choices.

1. Who we are

Emish Beauty Spa provides spa services (including facials, waxing, massage, body treatments) and beauty products, with branches in Dar es Salaam, Arusha and Dodoma.

Contact: WhatsApp / phone 0672 283 434, 0746 541 544 · www.emish.store

2. Data we collect

  • Identity & contact: name, phone number, email address (optional).
  • Orders: products ordered, delivery address, city, notes, order reference, payment status.
  • Bookings: service chosen, date and time, duration, booking reference, payment status, notes (e.g. preferences).
  • Account: login phone, password (stored hashed), referral code, role (customer or staff).
  • Payments: payment method and status. Card and mobile-money credentials are processed by Pesapal; we do not store full card numbers.
  • WhatsApp: messages you send to our business number (e.g. menu choices, order/booking requests) and replies we send (confirmations, payment links).
  • Technical: basic device/browser data and logs needed to run and secure the site (e.g. IP in server logs).

3. How we use your data

  • Process and deliver product orders and spa bookings.
  • Send payment links and confirm payment status via the website and WhatsApp/SMS where enabled.
  • Customer support and branch coordination.
  • Operate the referral/commission programme when you use or share a referral code.
  • Improve security, prevent fraud, and meet legal or regulatory requests.

We do not sell your personal data.

4. WhatsApp

If you message our WhatsApp Business number, we use that conversation to show menus, take order/booking details, send payment links, and confirm appointments. Message content is processed on our systems and via Meta’s WhatsApp Business tools under Meta’s terms. You can stop by simply not messaging us; for deletion of stored booking/order data linked to your phone, contact us using the details above.

5. Sharing with service providers

  • Pesapal — payment processing.
  • Hosting — website and API hosting providers.
  • SMS / WhatsApp providers — delivery of transactional messages when configured.
  • Professional advisers or authorities when required by law.

6. Retention

We keep order and booking records as long as needed for service, accounting, disputes and legal obligations, then delete or anonymise where practicable.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, and access controls for staff accounts. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a strong password and do not share OTPs or payment codes.

8. Your choices

  • Request access, correction, or deletion of your account or contact data where applicable.
  • Opt out of non-essential marketing messages (transactional order/booking messages may still be sent).

Contact us on the phone numbers above or via the website.

9. Children

Our online shop and booking tools are aimed at adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children for online accounts without appropriate consent.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the site or WhatsApp service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Governing context

Emish operates in Tanzania. This policy is intended to be transparent about our practices for customers and for platforms (including Meta) that require a public privacy policy URL.
